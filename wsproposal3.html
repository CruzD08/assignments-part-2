1. Project Description

What problem, business, or idea are you addressing?

This project focuses on creating a youth-centered travel agency website that highlights cultural festivals and meaningful travel experiences around the world. Many young adults are interested in attending international festivals and exploring new cultures, but travel websites often feel overwhelming, cluttered, or difficult to navigate.

This website aims to provide a clean, visually engaging, and user-friendly platform where young travelers can easily explore destinations, view travel packages, learn about cultural events, and request personalized itineraries. The goal is to create an intuitive and interactive front-end experience that makes planning travel feel exciting rather than stressful.

What inspired this idea?

This idea was inspired by my first trip to another continent, specifically South Korea. It became one of the most meaningful experiences of my life and motivated me to continue exploring different cultures and festivals around the world.

However, planning that first international trip required a lot of research, organizing, and comparing options. The process felt overwhelming at times, especially as a first-time traveler. That experience inspired me to design a travel agency platform that feels clear, organized, and easy to use, especially for young adults who may be planning their first big international adventure.

2. Purpose & Application

Who is the target audience?

The target audience for this website is young adults between the ages of 18 and 30 who are interested in cultural exploration, festivals, and international travel. This includes college students and young professionals who are looking for adventurous and meaningful travel experiences.

The website is especially designed for individuals who may be planning their first international trip and need a platform that feels simple, inspiring, and easy to navigate.

What problem does your site help solve?

Many travel websites feel overwhelming, crowded with information, or difficult to navigate. Young travelers often struggle to find clear information about festivals, destinations, and trip planning without feeling confused.

This website helps solve that problem by providing a clean layout, organized navigation, and interactive features that make exploring travel options simple and engaging. The goal is to make the experience of browsing trips feel exciting and motivating rather than stressful.

What real-world application does it have?

In a real-world setting, this website functions as a front-facing travel agency platform. Users can explore destinations, view travel packages, learn about cultural festivals, and submit requests for booking or personalized itineraries.

The site emphasizes responsive design, smooth navigation, and interactive elements that improve the overall user experience. It reflects how modern travel companies design visually appealing and user-friendly websites to attract and engage young travelers.

4. Page Descriptions

A short explanation of what each page does

Home

The Home page introduces the travel agency and its mission. It will feature a visually engaging layout with strong imagery from cultural festivals and travel destinations. Users will be able to navigate easily to other sections of the site through a clean and simple navigation bar. The page may include featured trips and call-to-action buttons that guide users to explore packages or festivals.

Packages

The Packages page displays available travel experiences and destinations in an organized layout. Users can browse different trips and click on individual packages to view more details. The page may include interactive elements such as hover effects, image galleries, or filtering options to improve user experience. Each package page will include a comment section where users can post messages related to the trip.

Festivals

The festivals page highlights important cultural events around the world. It will present information in a visually appealing format, possibly using images, cards, or sections that make it easy to read. Users can click on a festival to learn more or navigate to related travel packages.

Itinerary

The Itinerary page allows users to request a personalized travel plan. The page will include a structured form where users can input their travel preferences, such as destination, travel style, and number of days. Client-side form validation may be used to ensure users complete required fields before submitting.

Contact

The Contact page allows users to send general inquiries. It will include a contact form with clear input fields and a simple design to encourage communication.

5. Design Concept

How should the site look and feel?

What kind of images, colors, or layout will you use?

The website should feel exciting, inspiring, and easy to use. I want it to feel like a real travel agency, but also youthful and adventurous. The goal is for users to feel like they’re already ready to book that trip.

It will include images of cultural festivals, important landmarks, and meaningful travel moments. For colors, I’m thinking of using blue and orange. Blue for trust and calmness, and orange to add energy and excitement.

The layout will be clean and simple, with clear navigation and a design that works well on both desktop and mobile. The final look may change as the project develops, but the main focus is to keep it modern, organized, and visually engaging.

Results & Success

How will you know if your website is working?

What data or interaction will show success?

The website will be successful if users can move through the pages easily and understand how everything works without confusion. If visitors can explore packages, learn about festivals, and submit forms without issues, then the site is doing its job.

Success will also be shown if users interact with the platform, for example, clicking on packages, navigating between pages smoothly, and completing forms correctly. If the layout feels clear, responsive, and easy to use on both desktop and mobile, that will show that the website is functioning properly.
